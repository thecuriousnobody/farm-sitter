# Legal Positioning Notes

## Core Legal Identity

The Farm Sitter is a **certification and referral platform**, not a service provider, employer, or franchisor.

## Key Distinctions

### NOT an Employer
- Operators are independent business owners
- Platform does not control how/when work is performed
- No employment relationship with sitters
- Operators set their own pricing, schedules, terms

### NOT a Franchisor
- No exclusive territories
- No operational control requirements
- Brand licensing is optional, not required
- Affiliates operate under preference, not exclusivity
- Annual fee is for brand use and enhanced services, not a franchise fee
- Avoid franchise-triggering language in all copy and contracts

### NOT a Marketplace Controller
- Platform facilitates referrals, not bookings
- Booking occurs directly between operator and customer
- Platform does not take a cut of each transaction
- Customer relationship belongs to the operator

## What It IS

- A training and education provider
- A credentialing body
- A standards organization
- A referral and discovery platform
- An optional brand licensor
- A business support platform

## Required Disclaimers / Copy Guidelines

- Operators described as "independent" and "business owners"
- Territory language uses "preference" not "exclusive" or "protected"
- License language avoids "franchise" terminology
- Terms of service must clearly establish independent contractor / platform relationship
- No language implying employment, agency, or operational control

## Insurance and Liability

- Operators maintain their own liability insurance
- Platform verifies insurance status but does not provide coverage
- Background check requirements configurable by tier
- Platform liability limited to platform services, not field operations
