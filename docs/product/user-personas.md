# User Personas

## 1. Prospective Farm Sitter
Someone interested in starting or growing a farm-sitting business.

**Needs:**
- Understand the opportunity
- Learn pros/cons
- Compare packages
- Register interest
- Enroll in workshop
- Access onboarding and training

## 2. Registered Trainee
Someone who has paid for workshop/training.

**Needs:**
- Course access
- Schedule info
- Downloadable resources
- Progress tracking
- Post-workshop next steps

## 3. Credentialed Operator
Completed education/testing.

**Needs:**
- Profile management
- Badge/credential status
- Referral eligibility
- Compliance reminders
- Business tools
- Directory visibility (if eligible)

## 4. Licensed Affiliate
Using The Farm Sitter brand.

**Needs:**
- License status
- Territory settings
- Brand assets
- Priority referrals
- Compliance checklist
- Annual renewal workflow

## 5. Animal Owner / Farm Customer
Seeking a sitter for horses, hobby farm animals, dogs, poultry, cats, or mixed-property care.

**Needs:**
- Understand what farm sitting is
- Know whether a sitter is available nearby
- Submit inquiry
- Search by geography
- Review sitter type/status
- Get referred

## 6. Corporate Admin
Internal team managing operations.

**Manages:**
- Leads and workshop sales
- Trainee progression
- Credential records
- Directory placement
- Affiliate compliance
- Customer inquiries
- Territory conflicts
- Documents and renewals
