# Customer Journeys

## Journey A: Prospective Farm Sitter / Operator

1. User lands on "Become a Farm Sitter"
2. Reviews value proposition, pros/cons, and opportunity
3. Submits contact information
4. Sees training/workshop offer and pricing
5. Registers for paid in-person workshop
6. Gains access to digital training materials and resources
7. Attends workshop
8. Completes LMS-based modules/testing
9. Earns credential if requirements are met
10. May apply for referral listing
11. May later upgrade to licensed affiliate status

### Product Requirements from This Journey
- Lead capture
- Pricing reveal logic
- Registration and payment path
- LMS gateway/integration
- Post-event resource hub
- Credential record
- Referral eligibility logic
- Upgrade paths

## Journey B: Animal Owner / Customer

1. Customer lands on thefarmsitter.com
2. Learns what the service/category is
3. Registers or submits inquiry
4. Provides: name, contact info, zip/city/state, animal types, quantities, trip/care details
5. System identifies potential sitters by geography and status
6. Results prioritize:
   - Licensed affiliates with local priority
   - Then certified/referral-eligible sitters within expanded range
7. If no sitter is available → post to bulletin board
8. Customer is referred to sitter(s)
9. Booking generally occurs directly with sitter, not through platform
