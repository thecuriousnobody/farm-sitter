# Product Overview

## What Is The Farm Sitter?

The Farm Sitter is a national farm-sitter training, credentialing, referral, and optional brand-licensing platform built for rural and livestock care providers.

The platform serves a dual-sided ecosystem:
- **Primary paying customer**: the farm sitter / operator
- **Downstream demand driver**: the animal owner / farm client

## Core Functions

1. **Train and educate** aspiring farm sitters
2. **Credential and certify** qualified operators
3. **Support optional brand-affiliated operators**
4. **Help animal owners find** qualified farm sitters

## Three Operator Pathways

### A. Education Only
User registers, pays for training, attends workshop(s), and accesses course materials without becoming listed for referral.

### B. Credentialed Independent Operator
User completes training and testing, earns a Farm Sitting Credential, and may operate independently under their own business identity while benefiting from education, standards, badge usage, and optional referral services.

### C. Licensed Brand Affiliate
A qualified operator may optionally license The Farm Sitter brand and receive enhanced placement, territory preference, brand-use rights, and additional support, subject to annual fees and compliance requirements.

## What It Is NOT

- Not a full-service dispatch marketplace
- Not an employer of sitters
- Not a franchisor
- Not the direct provider of all local animal care services
- Not the party controlling every field-level customer engagement

## Long-Term Vision

Build a trusted, standardized, recognizable farm-sitting industry category. The national operating system for independent farm-sitting businesses combining training, credentialing, standards, directory discovery, onboarding, operator tools, territory logic, optional brand licensing, and future workflow automation.
