# Pricing and Service Logic

## Animal Care Tier System

From validated direct-service operations. Preserved as training content, operator toolkit, and future calculator engine.

### Tier 1 — Typical Pets & Light Care
**Animals:** Cats, chickens, ducks, fish, rabbits, caged pets, lizards
- 1 visit/day typical, sometimes 2
- Second visit may qualify for rate reduction

### Tier 2 — Herd-Style Farm Animals
**Animals:** Goats, sheep, llamas, alpacas, turkeys, exotic birds, similar group animals
- 30-60 minute visits
- Usually 2 visits/day
- Group care rather than named individual care
- Custom review if combined with higher tiers

### Tier 3 — Individualized Farm Animal Care
**Animals:** Horses, donkeys, mini horses, named/individualized herd animals, stalled animals
- 60-90 minute visits
- Cleaning, turnout, blanketing, routine handling
- Minimum 2 visits/day

### Tier 4 — Specialized Farm Care
**Services:** Milking, medication, rehab, time-sensitive care, exotic/highly specialized routines
- 60-90+ minutes
- No second-visit discount assumption
- Special/custom pricing

## Service Rules

- **Meet and greet required** for every first-time customer/sitter relationship
- **Overnight care**: flat-rate add-on
- **Travel/mileage**: flat-rate structures
- **Dog-only visits**: separate rules
- **Second-visit reduction**: applies in some tiers
- **Chore time**: pricing determinant
- **Custom review**: triggered when care falls outside normal assumptions
- **First-job time multiplier**: new worker estimate adjustment
- **Customer-estimated time input**: captured for comparison
- **Quote range**: plus/minus variance logic
- **Seasonal/complexity**: may affect pricing
